# Tilt

<p class="lede">Opens and closes your window blinds from your iPhone, Apple Home, Siri, and schedules that run on the controller itself.</p>

Tilt is a small battery-powered controller and a printed tilter that fits inside the headrail of ordinary horizontal
blinds. The wand still works by hand.

- Open, close, or set any angle, from the app, Apple Home or Siri
- Move gently over minutes, or follow sunrise and sunset
- Share with the people you live with
- No account, no cloud server, no tracking
